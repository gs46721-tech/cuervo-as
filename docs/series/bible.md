# Series bible (reference)

Working title for the reference episode: **Five Minutes**. One scene. The bible is only the facts that scene is allowed to use.

## Premise

Nia lets Cole sit through one evening in her apartment. They keep failing to start the thing they sat down to do.

## Continuity the scene must keep

- The location is Nia's living room. It is evening and both lamps are on.
- The television is off for the entire scene.
- Nia sits on the left cushion. Cole sits on the right. Neither stands.
- The remote is under Nia's cushion and is never produced.
- Costumes do not change inside the scene.
- There is no third person, phone call, or cutaway.

## Tone

Quiet, specific, a little mean, then a truce. Holds do the comedy. Nobody explains the joke.
